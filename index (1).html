About me

I’m a results-driven digital marketer with a background in graphic design and technical training. I build ad creatives in Photoshop, Illustrator and Canva, and I optimize digital campaigns so they reach the right people.

Before moving into marketing, I trained university students and school learners in computer skills. That experience taught me to explain things simply, which now helps me onboard clients and keep them in the loop.

I’m growing as an entrepreneur in the ICT and creative industry, and I enjoy turning ideas into polished digital work.
